About LKE Future

Our Mission & Commitment

Our Mission: Empower individuals to realize their full potential by providing transformative services, resources, and support that inspire growth, resilience, and purpose.

LKE Future LLC is committed to strengthening communities through quality, stable housing. We expand our portfolio with thoughtfully repaired, upgraded, and enhanced properties—without increasing costs for residents. We believe in creating sustainable homes that uplift residents while investing in long-term community vitality.

Beyond housing, we are scaling into:

  • Employment opportunities and skills development
  • Skilled trades training and career pathways
  • Construction contracting and property enhancement
  • Community economic strengthening
For veterans specifically, we recognize that stable, quality housing is foundational—but it is never sufficient alone. We address the unique challenges veterans face through integrated, supportive services embedded directly within our housing model.

Our Core Services

Comprehensive Support for Veteran Stability

Housing Onboarding & Orientation

Professional onboarding focused on housing stability, service needs, and personal goals. We facilitate smooth transitions into housing and establish supportive relationships.

Case Management & Coordination

Ongoing relationship management, service plan development, progress monitoring, and coordination with community partners (VA, mental health providers, employment services).

Trauma-Informed Service Delivery

Our team understands military culture and the complexity of reintegration. We serve veterans with respect, dignity, and awareness of trauma's impact on housing stability.

Community Partnership & Referrals

We actively build relationships with VA medical centers, veteran service organizations, employment agencies, and educational institutions to ensure comprehensive resource access.

Property Management & Maintenance

Professional management aligned with Housing Quality Standards, pest control, repairs, utilities management, and occupancy reporting for partner organizations.

Emergency Shelter Support

Rapid building maintenance and quick unit turnovers to support emergency shelter operations when needed, ensuring immediate veteran housing access.

Our Partnership Model

The Continuum of Care Approach

We do not operate in a silo. LKE Future aligns operations with local veteran service needs and establishes strong referral and support channels through three integrated pillars:

1. Service Provision

On-Site Case Management Support

Dedicated case management and residential service provider within partner housing properties, lightening administrative burden while ensuring quality, consistent service delivery.

2. Referral Partnerships

Strengthening the Ecosystem

Trusted referral partner coordinating directly with regional housing authorities, VA entities, and community agencies to receive referred candidates and strengthen the local continuum.

3. Integrated Stability

Housing + Support Services

Operate housing with embedded service delivery, reducing administrative friction and improving long-term outcomes through comprehensive on-site support.

VA Grant and Per Diem (GPD) Alignment

LKE Future is actively seeking partnership with HVAF (Housing VA for Veterans) to operate transitional housing in Muncie, Indiana under VA GPD funding. We coordinate seamlessly with:

  • HVAF (Housing VA for Veterans)
  • VA Medical Centers
  • Regional Coordinated Entry Systems (CES)
  • Local veteran service organizations

Rather than facilitating direct initial intake assessments internally, we ensure full compliance with VA GPD intake standards while providing high-quality residential care and transitional support on-site.
